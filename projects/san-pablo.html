San Pablo Driveway & Patio

A full-property pour — driveway, side walkway, and backyard patio, each finished with its own pattern.

Overview

One Property, Several Finishes

This San Pablo, CA project covered the whole property in a single job: a hexagonal stone-stamped driveway and entrance, a scored and stamped side walkway leading to the backyard, and a wood-stamped rear patio. Matching each area to its own pattern let us give the driveway a bold stone look up front while keeping the backyard warm and natural underfoot — all poured and finished by the same crew for a consistent result throughout.
