FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What's the difference between boom and line pumping?

Boom pumps use a hydraulic arm for reach over obstacles and large-volume pours; line pumps use flexible hose for tight or hard-to-reach sites. We'll recommend the right one for your project during the quote.

Do you provide the pump operator?

Yes — every pumping job includes an experienced operator, so you don't need to arrange separate labor for the pump itself.

How much concrete can a boom pump handle in a day?

It depends on site access and mix design, but our crews are equipped to handle both small residential pours and large commercial volumes in a single day.

How far in advance should I schedule a pump?

We recommend booking as soon as your pour date is set. Availability can get tight during peak season, so earlier scheduling gives you more flexibility.
