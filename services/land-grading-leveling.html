FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Why does grading matter before a concrete pour?

Proper grading prevents water pooling, uneven settling, and cracking down the line — it's the foundation for everything that comes after.

Can you fix drainage issues on an existing slab?

In many cases, yes. Regrading around an existing slab can redirect water away from your foundation.

How long does grading take before you can pour?

Most residential sites are ready within a day or two, depending on size and soil conditions.

Do you handle both small yards and large lots?

Yes — our equipment is suited for both tight residential yards and larger commercial lots.
